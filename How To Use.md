## How To Use Our Service

After you have installed our application from the Google Workspace Marketplace, navigate to a Google Doc, Google Slide, or Google Sheet of your choice. Select the text you would like to use our extension on. Hover over the "Extensions" menu, and you will see our add-on menu, titled "Transformations." Hover over this menu, and the side menu will open. There are four options that will appear.

# Capitalize All Words

When you click this, the first letter of every word in your selected text will become uppercase.

# Capitalize All Sentances

When you click this, the first letter of every sentance in your selected text will become uppercase.

# Make Uppercase

When you click this, every letter in your selected text will become uppercase.

# Make Lowercase

When you click this, every letter in your selected text will become lowercase.
